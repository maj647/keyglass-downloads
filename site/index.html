What it does

Three ways to listen, one answer.

Beat

Drop a file

Seven key detectors and five tempo detectors vote, section by section. Close calls are shown, never hidden.

Mic

Listen to the room

Pick any input, from the built-in mic to your audio interface. Settles in about 30 seconds.

Mac Audio

Hear what's playing

YouTube, Spotify, your DJ software: Keyglass listens before your speakers, on any output.

Tuning

Off 440? You'll know

Shows A4 in hertz and cents, and the nearest standard key when a beat sits between two.

Tag

Write it into the file

BPM and key tags that Serato, Rekordbox and Traktor read, and names like 94-Am-Title.mp3.

DAW

Send to Pro Tools

Push the key to Waves Tune or Auto-Tune on your record tracks, plus tap tempo when your ears disagree.
