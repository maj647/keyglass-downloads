Waitlist privacy notice

Last updated October 17, 2026

This notice covers the Keyglass waitlist and newsletter. Keyglass is made by Adil Majarali in Toronto, Ontario, Canada ("we").

What we collect

  • Your email address and, if you give it, your first name.
  • Your consent: the date and time you ticked the box, the wording you agreed to, where you signed up (for example the website or a printed card) and your email confirmation.
  • Basic email stats, like whether an email was opened or a link was clicked.

Why

To send you what you asked for: Keyglass news, beta invites, tips and offers, and the free gift. We never sell your information and we don't share it with advertisers.

Who handles it

Our email service, Kit (kit.com), stores the list and sends the emails for us. Kit is based in the United States, so your information may be stored and processed there and be subject to US law.

How long we keep it

While you're subscribed. After you unsubscribe we keep only a record of your consent and unsubscribe for up to 3 years, as Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) requires us to be able to show consent.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe any time with the one-click link at the bottom of every email.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete your information by emailing us. We'll reply within 30 days.
  • If you're unhappy with how we handled your information, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).

Contact

You must be 16 or older to join the list.
